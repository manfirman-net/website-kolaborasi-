Bahasa Inggris

Cardboard waste is one of the biggest environmental problems caused by online shopping and packaging. Used cardboard is often discarded when it is no longer needed. However, this thick cardboard is still strong and durable. One creative way to manage this waste is by transforming old cardboard into a multifunctional wall clock called ReClock Pro. This transformation shows how simple packaging waste can become a useful, high-value, and multifunctional product.

The transformation begins when used thick cardboard is collected and cut into the main pattern. The cardboard is measured to 60 cm in height and 50 cm in width for the front view, and 10 cm in thickness for the side view to make it sturdy. It is then separated into several small compartments for different functions. At this stage, the original function of the cardboard changes. Instead of being used for packaging, it becomes the strong frame for a new product.

The cut cardboard is combined and glued to form an octagonal frame. Through the process of arranging and attaching, several functions are created in one product. An analog clock with blue LED is installed on the top left, a printed digital calendar showing 10:24, Monday, 14 April 2025 is placed on the top right, a small shelf for a mini plant and a pen holder is formed on the middle left, a memo board with a note “Jangan lupa belajar!” is attached in the center, and a phone holder is created at the bottom. A QR code for website access and a warm LED strip on the back are also added.

As a result, old cardboard is transformed into a ReClock Pro that has multifunctional value. The product can be used as a wall clock, a calendar, a pen holder, a memo board, a phone holder, and a decoration with 60 x 60 cm size. This transformation reduces the amount of packaging waste and encourages the reuse of available materials. Therefore, recycling cardboard into a multifunctional wall clock is one creative way to support a more sustainable and productive environment.
